[ 03 ] FAQ

Questions, answered

What do you build?

Websites for businesses, brands and creators: landing pages, portfolios and multi-page sites, written as clean standalone HTML. I also design logos, posters and brand identities, and I rebuild Webflow designs.

How long does a website take?

A simple one-page site usually takes a few days, and a multi-page site takes one to two weeks. The real timeline depends mostly on how quickly I get your content and feedback.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the number of pages and the features you need. After a short chat about your goals, I send a fixed quote so there are no surprises.

What do you need from me to start?

Your business name, your logo if you have one, the text you want on the site, your images, and a few websites you like. If something is missing, I can help you write or source it.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every site I build is responsive, so it works on phones, tablets and desktops, and I test it before I hand it over.

Can you rebuild a design or Webflow template I like?

Yes. I rebuild Webflow designs and templates as fast, standalone HTML sites, including the animations and scroll effects.

How do I get started?

Message me on X and tell me about your project. I'll reply with a few questions and a quote, and we start once we agree on the scope and price.
